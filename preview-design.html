Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our editing workflow, turnaround times, and vertical delivery standards.

I have raw footage or a podcast, but I'm not sure what clips will perform best. Can you help? +

Yes! We review your full raw footage or long-form recordings, identify the highest-retention conversational moments, punchlines, and insights, and turn them into viral-ready 9:16 vertical cuts.

How do you optimize edits for vertical platforms (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)? +

Every platform has native UI overlays (like comment icons, audio bars, and account tags). We edit strictly within the 9:16 safety zones, use large centered captions, dynamic zoom cuts, and fast-paced B-roll inserts to maintain visual energy.

Can you also handle horizontal (16:9) YouTube videos when I'm ready? +

Absolutely! Our production pipeline is fully modular. We can deliver both full-length widescreen (16:9) YouTube videos with in-depth chaptering and derive 5-10 vertical short cuts from the exact same master file.

What is your typical turnaround time per cut? +

For short-form vertical reels, turnaround is typically 24 to 48 hours. We deliver timecoded review links via Frame.io, allowing you to leave exact timestamped feedback.

Simply click "Hire me" or reach out directly at contact@videoeditor.pro. We'll reply within 24 hours to review your footage and turnaround timeline.
